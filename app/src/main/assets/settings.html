FLOAT MODE (VOICE BUBBLE)

Tap the round float button next to the mic on the chat screen. A small bubble hovers over every app and listens. It also starts by itself whenever you open an app through Pragon.

"I'm home" or "Daddy's home" (with or without "Pragon") - start listening (bubble turns cyan)
"open Instagram", "close YouTube", "search YouTube for lofi", "go home", "volume up", "scroll down"
"Mute" - microphone fully off (tap the bubble to turn it back on)
"Goodbye" - closes float mode

Bubble: tap = listening on/off, drag = move, long-press = close. Needs the microphone, "Display over other apps", and the accessibility service (for closing apps).
